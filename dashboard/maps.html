About these maps

Surface. The fsaverage6 inflated cortex (40,962 vertices per hemisphere), shaded by sulcal depth (darker = sulcus). fsaverage6 is nested in fsaverage, so ROIs and maps from the analyses transfer exactly.

ROIs are the ones used in the analyses: Wang et al. (2015) maximum-probability retinotopic areas (IPS4 and IPS5 merged); V1–V3 split into a fovea band (0.5–1.5°, the task ring) and a periphery band (3–7.5°, the noise annulus) by the Benson (2014) template (the per-subject pRF versions are not shown); the 12 Schaefer (2018) 17-network networks analyzed; and the selected Glasser HCP-MMP1 areas. "Load effect" colors each ROI by its group load effect (fitted change from 10% to 46% load, both hemispheres averaged, 8 subjects), the same numbers as on the fMRI tab.

Load-effect map. For each subject and vertex, the 7 load betas from the original GLMs (5 mm smoothing) were regressed on load and expressed as the fitted change from 10% to 46%; the map shows the mean across the 8 subjects. "Only p < .05" hides vertices with |t(7)| < 2.36, uncorrected for the number of vertices, so treat it as descriptive.
